SCE Working Group 1 · Language & Formal Semantics

Economic models that machines can read, manipulate, and verify.

A working group of the Society for Computational Economics building a shared, formal language for dynamic economic models and their computational solutions.

Co-chaired by Akshay Shanker and Matt McKay

Formed at CEF 2026, Venice · Sponsored by SCE and partnered with Econ-ARK and QuantEcon

01 · Why

The problem

Structural economics has no standard, shareable representation of a dynamic model together with its computational solution. Models can't travel between toolkits, and results are hard to cross-verify. AI raises the stakes: language models already write modelling code, but without an explicit statement of what a model is, checking their output falls back on the economist.

The vision

A formal account of what a model file means, stated by the people who build the toolkits — so a model written in one system can be checked, translated, and reproduced in another, by humans and machines alike. WG1 is one of three working groups formed to address the "Tower of Babel problem" in computational economics.

02 · Projects
Project A

The Semantics of Economic Models

Each toolkit team — Dynare, HARK, VFI Toolkit, and others — states formally what its model files mean, publishing a per-toolkit paper.

→ Toward a journal special issue

Project B

A Community Library of Baseline Models

A standardized repository of the community's baseline models in existing notation, with each toolkit's treatment documented side by side.

→ Starts immediately · test corpus for Project A
